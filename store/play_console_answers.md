# Google Play Console answer guide

## App access

No account, login, or special access is required.

## Ads

- The app contains no advertising.
- Answer **No** to advertising declarations.

## Data safety

- Data collected: **None**
- Data shared: **None**
- Encrypted in transit: Not applicable; no user data is transmitted.
- Account deletion: Not applicable; no account is created.
- Data deletion: Available by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

## App category

- Primary category: **Games**
- Secondary category: **Puzzle**

## Target audience

Complete Google Play's current target audience form using the final publisher policy. The app is a non-social puzzle game with no advertising or purchases.

## Content rating

Cubora contains no sexual content, gambling, violence, profanity, drugs, or user-generated content. Complete the interactive questionnaire based on the actual in-app experience.

## Financial features

The app has no real-money purchases or external payments. Gems and rewards are earned only within the game.

## Privacy policy

Publish `store/privacy_policy.html` at a public URL and enter that URL in the Play Console. Replace the placeholder support email before release.
