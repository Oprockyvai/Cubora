## App name

Cubora: Block Puzzle

## Short description

A colorful offline block puzzle with worlds, daily challenges, rewards, and relaxing neon gameplay.

## Full description

Fit colorful blocks onto the 8 x 8 board and clear complete rows and columns. Play classic mode, take on timed challenges, earn gems, unlock worlds, and enjoy a neon puzzle experience made for quick sessions.

Features:
- Offline block puzzle gameplay
- Classic and level modes
- Daily challenge and reward systems
- Achievements and progress tracking
- Four colorful worlds
- Native haptic feedback
- Local score, gem, and preference saving
- No ads, account login, or online permissions

Cubora is designed for relaxed play. Its game assets and code are bundled with the app, so gameplay remains available without an internet connection.
