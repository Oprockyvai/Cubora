Cubora - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Cubora ("the app") is a block puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Cubora does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, does not ask for personal details and does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, scores, gems, settings, display name and avatar) is saved locally on your device so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any Android permissions.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Cubora contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking services.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before the change is released.
