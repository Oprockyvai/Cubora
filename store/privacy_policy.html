Block Clash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Block Clash ("the app") is a block puzzle game that plays fully offline. In short: the game itself does not collect your personal information or send your progress anywhere; it stores everything on your device. Ads shown in the app are provided by third-party advertising partners, described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information ourselves. The app requires no account and asks for no personal details. When ads are shown, our advertising partners (see below) may collect certain device and usage information, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Information stored on your device

Your local game data (level progress, high scores, gems, unlocked themes, sound/haptics/motion preferences, tutorial state, and local profile name/avatar) is saved strictly on your device so your progress persists across sessions. You can delete this data anytime via in-game Settings ("Reset all data") or by clearing app storage / uninstalling.

Permissions

The app does not request sensitive device permissions (no precise location, camera, microphone, or contacts). It uses network access to load ads, and the Android advertising ID permission for advertising. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Block Clash shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads using Google AdMob, with Unity Ads participating through AdMob mediation. These partners process data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads and you can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy & ad choices, and also reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). The app contains no other analytics or tracking services.

Children

Block Clash is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we add or change features that affect your data, this policy will be updated prior to release.
