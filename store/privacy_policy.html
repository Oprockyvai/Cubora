Privacy Policy for Cubora

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Cubora is an offline block puzzle game. The app does not require an account and does not send gameplay or personal information to a server.

Information stored on the device

Cubora saves game progress, scores, gems, preferences, achievements, and related settings locally on the device using local WebView storage. This information remains on the device and is not transmitted by the app.

Data collection and sharing

Cubora does not include advertising, analytics, payment services, account systems, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Permissions

Cubora does not require location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or internet permissions for gameplay.

Children's privacy

Cubora does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Progress is stored only on the device.

Data deletion

You can delete locally saved game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
