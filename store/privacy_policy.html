Block Clash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Block Clash ("the app") is an offline block puzzle game. In short: Block Clash does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, share, or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app requires no account, asks for no personal details, and makes no network requests for telemetry, analytics, or advertising.

Information stored on your device

Your local game data (level progress, high scores, gems, unlocked themes, sound/haptics/motion preferences, tutorial state, and local profile name/avatar) is saved strictly on your device so your progress persists across sessions. You can delete this data anytime via in-game Settings ("Reset all data") or by clearing app storage / uninstalling.

Permissions

The app does not request sensitive device permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or advertising ID).

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Block Clash contains zero ads, zero analytics SDKs, and zero third-party tracking services.

Children

Because Block Clash collects no data from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If future versions introduce any online or data-collecting feature, this policy will be updated prior to release.
